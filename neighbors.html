How Rindge compares

In brief

Each town sets its own limits. The proposed limits would be higher than both towns shown here, and this page doesn’t pretend otherwise.

The goal is not to outdo our neighbors. It is to give the limits Rindge voters chose in 2012 the same buying power they had then, and no more. In 2012, Rindge’s $35,000 and $49,000 (pending fact check) were worth what $51,110 and $71,554 are worth today. See the math.

Peterborough’s page shows its current limits. It does not say when those income limits were adopted, so no adoption year is given here. Other nearby towns are left out because we could not confirm their current figures and dates from official town sources.
