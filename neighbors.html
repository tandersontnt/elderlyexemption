“DRA 2024” marks a value taken from the NH Department of Revenue Administration’s Exemptions & Tax Credits Summary for tax year 2024, the latest it has posted (checked Oct. 11, 2026). A town may have changed a value since then. “Not found” means we could not locate the vote that set the current figures.

Winchendon and Ashburnham, Mass., also border Rindge but are left out because Massachusetts uses a different state law (Clause 41C, M.G.L. c. 59, § 5), so the figures aren’t comparable.

The goal is not to outdo our neighbors. It is to give the limits Rindge voters chose in 2012 the same buying power they had then, and no more. In 2012, Rindge’s $35,000 and $49,000 were worth what $51,110 and $71,554 are worth today. See the math.

The proposal changes only the income limits. Rindge’s asset limit ($150,000) and exemption amounts ($100,000 / $140,000 / $200,000) would stay the same.
