In brief

These neighbors asked to be listed when they used the Add your support form. Being listed here is not signing the petition; only signatures on the paper petition count.

The count includes everyone who has signed up. Names appear only for people who ticked “Show my name publicly as a supporter,” and only after review and approval. We show name and town only, never an address, phone number or email.
