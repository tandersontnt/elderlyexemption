Sources & data

Sources and data

In brief

Every figure on this site comes from one data file, and each figure lists its source.

Download the data

  • data.csv: every figure, one row each, with its label, value, status (official, derived, estimate, plan or unconfirmed) and source.
  • data.json: the same figures plus the source list and vote table. The site is built from this file.

“Derived” means arithmetic on official figures, shown on the Inflation math and Proposal & cost pages. “Estimate” means it depends on an assumption. “Plan” means a proposal or a scheduled date that is not yet official.

Source documents

Where a document is public and we have a copy, it is posted here next to a link to the original. Older Rindge reports (before 2010) link to the Internet Archive scans. Copies of state laws are plain-text snapshots; the official pages control.
