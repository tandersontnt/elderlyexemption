Vote history

Rindge has voted on this exemption ten times

In brief

Voters adopted it in 1976, expanded it in 1990, 2006 and 2012, and rejected a cut in 2022.

  • 224–771976: adopted (Art. 39)
  • 425–1061990: raised, by petition (Art. 27)
  • 1,267–1822006: doubled after the revaluation (Art. 27)
  • 615–4922012: today’s income limits set, by petition (Art. 20)
  • 601–3952022: cut rejected (No–Yes, Art. 11)
