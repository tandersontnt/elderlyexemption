Recipients

Who gets the exemption, 2020 to 2025

In brief

91 Rindge homeowners received the elderly exemption in 2020, and 52 did in 2025. [Source: Rindge report 2020; Rindge report 2025, MS-1 reports]

  • 91Homeowners receiving the elderly exemption, 2020
  • 52Homeowners receiving the elderly exemption, 2025

“Tax shifted” is the assessed value exempted times that year’s total tax rate: the tax those homeowners did not pay, which everyone else’s rate covered. It moves tax between owners; it does not change how much the town raises. The 2025 rate fell because of the revaluation, so the 2025 dollar figure is not directly comparable with earlier years. [Source: Research notes: 2025 town tax and elderly exemption by year; calculation]

2025 by age

20 ages 65 to 74, 13 ages 75 to 79 and 19 age 80 and older, with $6,559,100 of assessed value exempted in all. [Source: Rindge report 2025, pdf p.91]
