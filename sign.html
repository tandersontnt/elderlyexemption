Where to sign

Sign the petition in person

In brief

The petition is on paper only. There is no online signing. State law requires the signatures of 25 registered voters, handed to the Select Board by Tuesday, Jan. 12, 2027. [Source: RSA 39:3; RSA 40:13]

Who can sign: registered voters of the Town of Rindge. Sign in ink with your usual signature, and print your name and Rindge street address.

Can’t get out? Request a home visit and someone will bring the paper petition to you.

Collecting signatures yourself

Download the printable petition (PDF). It carries the full article text on every sheet, as signers must see what they are signing. Leave the primary petitioner line blank unless you have agreed who that is. Return completed sheets to the organizer, not to the town.

The PDF is marked DRAFT until the article text is final. Do not collect signatures on a draft sheet.
