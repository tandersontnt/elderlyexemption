How to sign

A resident’s guide to the March 2027 petition · Not yet filed

Where to sign

Sign the petition in person

In brief

The petition is on paper only. There is no online signing. State law requires the signatures of 25 registered voters, handed to the Select Board by Tuesday, Jan. 12, 2027. [Source: RSA 39:3; RSA 40:13]

Who can sign: registered voters of the Town of Rindge. Sign in ink with your usual signature, and print your name and Rindge street address.

Places and times

I’ll be at Dunkin’ in Rindge with the paper petition at these times:

  • Saturday, Dec. 12, 1 to 2 p.m.
  • Sunday, Dec. 13, 1 to 2 p.m.

Can’t make it? Request a home visit and I’ll bring the paper petition to you. It carries the full article text on every sheet, so you can see exactly what you are signing.

Handing it in

  1. Petitions go to the Select Board or any one member. I aim to hand them in by Tuesday, Jan. 5, 2027, a week before the Tuesday, Jan. 12, 2027 deadline.
  2. I will keep a copy and get a dated receipt.
  3. The town clerk may check that each signer is a registered voter.

[Source: RSA 39:3; organizer's plan]
