Limits over time

The exemption, the income limits and the asset limit since 1976

In brief

Each step on these charts is a town vote. The income limits have not moved since 2012, and the exemption amounts and asset limit have not moved since 2006.

  • $35,000Income limit, single (set March 2012, still in effect)
  • $49,000Income limit, married couple (set March 2012, still in effect)
  • $150,000Asset limit, not counting the home (single; married combined is the same)

* The 2020 MS-1 lists $50,000, which appears to be a reporting error; the 2012 vote and 2025 MS-1 both say $49,000. [Source: Rindge report 2020, 2020 MS-1, pdf p.70] [Source: Rindge report 2012; Rindge report 2025, 2012 Art. 20; 2025 MS-1, pdf p.91]

The 1983 limit of $30,000 excluded the residence and was lower than the $50,000 “assets of any kind” limit adopted in 1978. The pre-1978 limit of $35,000 is the limit named in the 1978 article; no 1976 document states it. A dash means that vote did not set this item (or the town report summary does not state it); the earlier amount is assumed to carry forward, and the charts draw it that way. 2012 limits are still in effect today. Sources for each vote are on the Votes page.
