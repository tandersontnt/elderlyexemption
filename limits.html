Limits over time

The exemption, the income limits and the asset limit since 1976

In brief

Each step on these charts is a town vote. The income limits have not moved since 2012, and the exemption amounts and asset limit have not moved since 2006.

  • $35,000Income limit, single (set March 2012, still in effect)
  • $49,000 (pending fact check)Income limit, married couple (set March 2012, still in effect)Pending
  • $150,000Asset limit, not counting the home (single; married combined is the same)

The 1983 limit of $30,000 excluded the residence and was lower than the $50,000 “assets of any kind” limit adopted in 1978. The pre-1978 limit of $35,000 is the limit named in the 1978 article; no 1976 document states it. A dash means that vote did not set this item (or the town report summary does not state it); the earlier amount is assumed to carry forward, and the charts draw it that way. 2012 limits are still in effect today.
